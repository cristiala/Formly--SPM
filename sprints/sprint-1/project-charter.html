Scope boundaries

In scope

Real-time feedback; gives tips in real time on exercise form depending on the exercise.

Out of scope

No coaching videos or tutorials, as our research found these features to be redundant and too expensive to implement.

Assumptions

  • User does not have a lot of gym experience.
  • Users have devices with functional cameras.
  • Users are willing to grant necessary app permissions for video.
  • Users prefer immediate feedback.
  • Users may not have access to or feel comfortable working with a personal trainer.
