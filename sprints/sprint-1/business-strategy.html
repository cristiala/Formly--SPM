Business Strategy

Connecting accessible exercise-form feedback to Formly’s proposed capabilities.

Business objective

Formly’s proposed business objective is to make timely exercise-form feedback more accessible to people with limited gym experience, particularly those who may not have access to or feel comfortable working with a personal trainer. The intended value is to help users recognize potential form issues during a workout and understand how to adjust their movements.

Strategy-to-project chain

Formly’s proposed approach is a camera-based application that uses computer vision to analyze exercise movements and provide immediate, exercise-specific feedback. This connects the business objective of accessible guidance to the project’s core capabilities: camera input, movement analysis, and timely corrective cues.

The strategy focuses on feedback about the user’s own movements. The intended outcome is guidance that users can understand and apply during their workout.

The initial scope prioritizes these capabilities. Coaching videos and tutorials are outside the proposed scope, while a chatbot and persistent video storage are deferred. This keeps the first version focused on evaluating whether its core feedback is useful and technically feasible. Scope boundaries and planning assumptions are maintained in the Project Charter.

Success measures

Software quality

  1. Performance efficiency: Measure how quickly Formly gives feedback and how much battery and memory it uses.
  2. Compatibility: Check that Formly works with the supported devices, operating systems, and cameras.
  3. Usability: Check whether beginners can set up the camera and follow the feedback without help.
  4. Reliability: Measure how quickly Formly recovers when the camera or body tracking stops working.
  5. User feedback: Ask users to rate the guidance, suggest improvements, and say whether they would use Formly again.
