Sprint 3

Documents the evidence, competitive research, change notes, risks, field notes, and contradictions for the next phase of the project.

October 8 - October 20

Evidence log

One field interview led to a hands-on review of Hevy.

Source Type Key takeaway Document
Interview X Customer X rewatched a Romanian deadlift clip but still could not tell how to correct his form. Recording himself felt inconvenient and confusion added about 10-15 minutes to a workout. —
Hevy teardown Competitive review Hevy logs workouts well and offers free exercise videos. The route to guidance was less intuitive, and the app did not give live feedback on the user's movement. EP Doc

Competitive Landscape

The team compared Hevy's workout logging and exercise videos with Formly's proposed form feedback. The interview and teardown show why seeing a demonstration may still leave a user unsure about their own movement.

Product Strength Gap
Hevy Useful free library, smooth workout logging, videos, polished UI, and no ads in the free experience No live form correction; the path to guidance was not intuitive

Change note

What changed: The team added evidence that feedback about a user's own movement may matter beyond showing exercise videos.

Why: The field interview and Hevy review showed that a video can be useful while still leaving a user unsure how to correct their form.

Impact: A next test could check whether timely cues help users adjust their form and whether camera setup is practical during a workout.

Candidate risk entries

  • Pose detection: Lighting or camera angle may reduce accuracy. Suggested owner: Technical lead. Trigger to watch: Repeated tracking failures in testing. Possible mitigation: Test calibration prompts and retry guidance.
  • Setup friction: Users may abandon a form check if positioning a phone interrupts their workout. Suggested owner: Product lead. Trigger to watch: Users skip setup in a gym trial. Possible mitigation: Test fewer setup steps and hands-free guidance.
  • Willingness to pay: A capable free workout tracker may make a second subscription hard to justify. Suggested owner: Business lead. Trigger to watch: Future interviewees reject the proposed price. Possible mitigation: Test pricing and Formly's value as a companion to existing trackers.

Field notes

Participant X (one interview): X rewatched a Romanian deadlift video about three times but still could not tell what to change. He tried recording himself and once asked another gym-goer for help, but found phone setup inconvenient. He spent $0 on form help and estimated that the confusion added 10-15 minutes to his workout.

Contradiction memo

Key contradiction: An exercise video does not always help a user understand whether their own movement is correct. X rewatched one several times and still could not identify what to change.

Recommendation: Test feedback on the user's movement while keeping setup simple enough for use during a workout.

Change log

  • Since Sprint 2: The team added one field interview and a hands-on Hevy teardown.
  • Why: The findings clarified the gap between watching an exercise and correcting your own form.
  • Affected: The Sprint 3 summary, candidate risks, and teardown document.
