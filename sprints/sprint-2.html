Business Case

Value analysis and go/no-go recommendation.

Formly addresses the need for accessible and timely exercise-form guidance for people with limited gym experience, especially users who may not have access to or feel comfortable working with a personal trainer. Our Sprint 1 research found that users value immediate feedback and that passive workout videos and tutorials do not provide the personalized guidance users need while actively exercising.

Formly's proposed solution is a camera-based application that uses computer vision to analyze a user's exercise movements and provide immediate, exercise-specific feedback. The initial product will focus on camera input, movement analysis, and real-time corrective cues while keeping coaching videos and tutorials outside the initial scope and deferring features such as a chatbot and persistent video storage.

Based on the user need identified in Sprint 1 and the project effort, staffing, and cost estimates developed during Sprint 2, our recommendation is to proceed with the project while continuing to evaluate technical feasibility, feedback accuracy, project cost, and user experience.

((O + 4M + P) / 6), and why we chose M		 (360 + 4(2,760) + 3,672) ÷ 6 = 2,512 hours. We chose 2,760 hours as our most likely value because it represents the middle Story Point estimate using our assumed velocity of 20 points per sprint.
Cost range and loaded rate used According to the Bureau of Labor statistics, the median annual wage for a software developer was $135,980 in May 2025. Assuming a 20% overhead then the loaded rate would be $65.37 an hour × 0.20 = 13.07, so $65.37 + $13.07 = $78.44 an hour. The cost range would then be:

• Expected: 2,512 hrs × 78.44 = $197,041.28
