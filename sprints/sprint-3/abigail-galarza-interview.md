## 2. Last-time story (4 minutes)

Interviewer: Can you walk me through the last time you checked whether you were doing an exercise correctly?

Tag: BEHAVIOR - describes a usual practice, not a specific last-time event.

## 3. Alternatives (3 minutes)

Interviewer: What other methods have you tried to check your form, and why did you stop using them?

X: I stopped relying on YouTube videos because a lot of people contradict themselves, so I’ve been confused many times trying to differentiate what’s right from wrong because social media has a lot of different opinions.

Tag: BEHAVIOR - stopped relying on YouTube. OPINION - views online advice as contradictory/confusing.

## 4. Switching story (4 minutes)

Interviewer: What made you start using the QR codes on the machines? Are you still using it? If not, why?

X: I started using the QR codes because they provide instructions from the manufacturer, so I assumed they were correct. Though I slowly stopped using them, they still helped me in the beginning.

Tag: BEHAVIOR - started and gradually stopped using QR codes. OPINION - assumed manufacturer instructions were correct and found them helpful.

## 5. Money and time (2 minutes)

Interviewer: How much time or money have you actually spent on checking or improving your form recently?

X: I’ve spent around $800 on personal trainers, trying out different trainers to see what worked best for me and helped improve my form. Eventually, I decided to go solo and work on improving my form on my own.

Tag: BEHAVIOR - reports ~$800 spent, trying different trainers, then training independently. Time period not specified.
