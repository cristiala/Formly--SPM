# Formly Team - Interview X

This document records the team's interview with anonymized participant X. Answers are paraphrased from the interview notes.

## 1. Screen (1 minute)

**Interviewer:** What do you currently use to help you check or improve your exercise form?

**X:** He uses the free Hevy app. He said it gives him a routine, and each exercise has a short video clip showing how to do it.

## 2. Last time story (4 minutes)

**Interviewer:** Can you walk me through the last time you checked whether you were doing an exercise correctly?

**X:** Earlier that week, Hevy gave him a routine with Romanian deadlifts, which he had done only a few times. He watched the clip of a person bending over while keeping their back straight and tried to copy it. He felt the effort in his lower back instead of his hamstrings. After rewatching the clip about three times, he still could not tell what he was doing wrong. He planned to look for an alternative exercise in a future session.

## 3. Alternatives (3 minutes)

**Interviewer:** What other methods have you tried to check your form, and why did you stop using them?

**X:** He tried recording himself on his phone, but propping it on a water bottle and watching the recording in the gym felt inconvenient. He once asked another gym-goer to check his form but did not want to take much of that person's time. He now relies on the app's videos because they are easier to use, even though they do not always help him feel that he is doing the exercise correctly.

## 4. Switching story (4 minutes)

**Interviewer:** What made you start using the Hevy app? Are you still using it? If not, why?

**X:** He started using Hevy because he wanted a plan and did not know what to do at the gym. He still uses its plans and usually watches the video when the app suggests an unfamiliar variation. He can see the movement in the video but, depending on the exercise, cannot tell whether his own movement is correct.

## 5. Money and time (2 minutes)

**Interviewer:** How much time or money have you actually spent on checking or improving your form recently?

**X:** He spends $0 on form checking, but spends time at the gym rewatching the roughly 10-second clips and trying to work out the movement. He estimated that the confusion adds about 10-15 minutes to his workout.

## 6. Close (1 minute)

**Interviewer:** Is there someone you know who has tried a similar method or something different?

**X:** He knows people who have used a trainer. They recommended that he try one, and he has considered it.
