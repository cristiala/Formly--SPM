# Formly Team - Interview X

This document records the team's interview with anonymized participant X. Answers are paraphrased from the interview notes.

Behavior tags describe X's account of specific events and actions, including his immediate response. Opinion tags describe broader judgments, preferences, or possibilities. Both come from X's account, not direct observation.

## 1. Screen (1 minute)

**Interviewer:** What do you currently use to help you check or improve your exercise form?

**X - Behavior:** He reported using the free Hevy app for a routine and watching the short video clip provided for each exercise.

## 2. Last time story (4 minutes)

**Interviewer:** Can you walk me through the last time you checked whether you were doing an exercise correctly?

**X - Behavior:** Earlier that week, Hevy gave him a routine with Romanian deadlifts, which he had done only a few times. He watched the clip of a person bending over while keeping their back straight and tried to copy it. He felt the effort in his lower back instead of his hamstrings, rewatched the clip about three times, and still could not tell what he was doing wrong. He decided to look for an alternative exercise in a future session.

## 3. Alternatives (3 minutes)

**Interviewer:** What other methods have you tried to check your form, and why did you stop using them?

**X - Behavior:** He tried recording himself on his phone, once asked another gym-goer to check his form, and now relies on the app's videos.

**X - Opinion:** He said propping his phone on a water bottle and watching the recording in the gym felt inconvenient. He did not want to take much of the other person's time. The app's videos felt easier to use, though they did not always help him feel that he was doing the exercise correctly.

## 4. Switching story (4 minutes)

**Interviewer:** What made you start using the Hevy app? Are you still using it? If not, why?

**X - Behavior:** He started using Hevy, still uses its plans, and usually watches the video when the app suggests an unfamiliar variation.

**X - Opinion:** He wanted a plan because he did not know what to do at the gym. He can see the movement in the video but, depending on the exercise, cannot tell whether his own movement is correct.

## 5. Money and time (2 minutes)

**Interviewer:** How much time or money have you actually spent on checking or improving your form recently?

**X - Behavior:** He reported spending $0 on form checking and rewatching the roughly 10-second clips at the gym. He estimated that trying to work out the movement adds about 10-15 minutes to his workout.

## 6. Close (1 minute)

**Interviewer:** Is there someone you know who has tried a similar method or something different?

**X - Opinion:** He has considered trying a trainer after people he knows who have used one recommended it.
