Contradiction memo

Key contradiction: Our team assumed that Formly users would have limited gym experience. However, Participant Y works out independently and still uses mirrors and gym machine QR codes for guidance. This raises the question of whether Formly could also benefit people who already have gym experience.

Recommendation: Test Formly with a diverse range of users, including beginners, experienced gym-goers, and people with disabilities, to gather feedback on their different needs and determine how the app can provide effective and accessible exercise guidance for everyone.

Change log
